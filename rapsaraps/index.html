Our Story

Food Made with Heart & Passion

Rapsarap's Homemade Food Traiding started as a simple love for authentic Filipino flavors — the kind of food that reminds you of home, of family lunches, and of flavors that linger long after the meal is done.

Every jar, every bottle, every batch is handcrafted with the finest local ingredients, no preservatives, and no compromise. From fiery chilly garlic to creamy peanut butter and tangy atsara — each product carries the soul of genuine Filipino cooking.

Because great food deserves to be shared. Rapsarap's Homemade Food Traiding is more than a brand — it's a promise of quality, authenticity, and unforgettable taste in every spoonful.
